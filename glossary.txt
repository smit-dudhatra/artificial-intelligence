AI slop

AI slop is low-quality, mass-produced digital content created using generative artificial intelligence with little to no human effort, editing, or meaningful purpose.Similar to online email spam, the term is used in a disapproving way to describe content that floods social media feeds, search engine results, and comment sections. Most users on r/aiwars agree that the term refers broadly to uninspired or low-effort algorithmic output designed primarily to chase clicks and engagement, though opinions differ on whether it is sometimes used as a generic buzzword to dismiss all AI art. The word "slop" was named the 2025 Word of the Year by Merriam-Webster and the Macquarie Dictionary.Common Types and Signs of AI SlopText: Repetitive phrasing, overly formal or generic wording, and shallow arguments that lack original human perspective.Images: Distorted anatomy (like extra fingers), weirdly smooth plastic textures, and nonsensical background text.Videos: Absurd scenarios (like cats in rainbow goo), robotic voiceovers, and unnatural movements designed to trigger algorithm views on platforms like TikTok and Instagram
